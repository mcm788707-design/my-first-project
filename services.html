FACILITY SERVICES

Integrated Facility Management (IFM)

Coordinated services that help keep buildings and workplaces functional, safe, compliant, and presentable. We work alongside your internal administration team to establish site priorities and align dedicated technical and housekeeping support.

  • Facility operations and administration
  • Planned preventative maintenance (PPM)
  • Vendor and specialized contractor coordination

TECHNICAL OPERATIONS

Technical & Maintenance Services

Reliable technical support for the physical infrastructure and critical MEP systems that power your facility. Our certified team covers electrical distribution, plumbing, fabrication, painting, and civil structural works.

  • Electrical panels and distribution circuits
  • Plumbing networks and sanitaryware
  • Carpentry, painting, and architectural fabrication
  • Civil repairs, tiling, and marble polishing
  • Toughened glass, safety vinyl, and film applications
